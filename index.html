How to Use the Text Formatter

Our tool simplifies everyday text formatting jobs. Simply paste your text into the box above to immediately see the total word and character counts. Use the utility actions to transform your text:

  • UPPERCASE: Converts all input letters into capital letters.
  • lowercase: Changes all letters into regular lowercase format.
  • Fix Extra Spaces: Strips trailing white spaces and shrinks multiple consecutive line/word spaces down to a single clean space.
  • Copy & Clear: Move your formatted output immediately to your clipboard or wipe the interface clean to start fresh.
